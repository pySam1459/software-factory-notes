# Matt Pocock's software factory course

- Source: https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2115538750147023323 (28 Oct 2026). Paid course, starts 9 Nov 2026, two weeks: https://www.aihero.dev/s/wS5YtK
- Thesis: "slow down to speed up". Brakes (constraints) come before the accelerator; without them a factory is a "slop cannon".
- Topics: automated review, codebase design, DDD and ubiquitous language, mutation testing, feedback loops; then a working factory, system design, infra planning; you leave with a factory spec to hand to an agent.
